Privacy.

Last updated: May 2026

Who we are

Presso is a personalized news briefing app operated from Vienna, Austria. We are responsible for the processing of your personal data as described in this policy.

Website: presso.at

What data we collect

When you use Presso, we collect account data such as your email address, user ID, display name, and, when provided by a sign-in provider, profile name or avatar URL.

We collect briefing preferences such as selected topics, selected country or region, language, local briefing time, onboarding status, notification preference, and playback preference.

We process app functionality data such as generated briefing transcripts, audio files, opened briefings, subscription entitlement status, purchase restoration status, and technical request data such as IP address, device or browser information, app version, requested URL, timestamp, and server logs.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use personal data for cross-app tracking or third-party advertising.

Why we collect it

We use your data to create and deliver your daily briefing, authenticate your account, keep you signed in, personalize topic and country selection, provide audio playback, schedule optional local reminders, manage subscriptions, restore purchases, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and provide support.

The legal basis for this processing is performance of the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), your consent where required (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and our legitimate interest in operating, securing, and improving Presso (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

AI and audio processing

Presso does not operate its own AI model. To generate concise news briefings, Presso may send public news headlines, public article summaries, source links, selected topics, language, country or region preference, and briefing instructions to third party AI and text-to-speech providers.

We do not intentionally send your email address, payment information, or account password to AI providers. We use third party AI and speech services only to provide the briefing feature, not to build an advertising profile or track you across apps and websites.

How long we keep it

We retain account data and preferences for as long as your account is active. Briefing transcripts and audio files are retained while needed to provide current and archived briefings. Technical logs are retained only as long as necessary for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and legal compliance.

If you delete your account in the app, we delete your account data, preferences, saved briefings, and associated audio where technically possible, except where we are required to retain limited records by law or for payment, tax, fraud prevention, or security reasons.

Third party services

Presso uses third party services to operate the app, including Supabase for authentication, database, and storage; RevenueCat and the Apple App Store or Google Play Store for subscriptions and purchase restoration; Google and Apple for optional social sign-in; Google Gemini or similar AI services for summarization; text-to-speech services for audio generation; and hosting, API, security, email, and infrastructure providers.

These providers may process personal data only as needed to provide their services, secure the app, comply with legal obligations, or support billing and subscriptions. We require appropriate safeguards from service providers where required by law.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can also object to processing, request data portability, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@presso.at.

Cookies

The Presso website does not set its own cookies. The current mobile app uses local device storage for login sessions and onboarding progress, but the reviewed app code does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

Some hosting, security, API, authentication, payment, or infrastructure providers may process technical connection data such as IP address, device and browser information, requested URL, timestamp, and server logs to deliver the service securely.

App cookie and storage overview

The following overview reflects the current implementation of the reviewed mobile app.

Technology Purpose Retention
App owned cookies None found in the reviewed app code. Not applicable
Supabase Auth session storage Stores authentication session data such as access and refresh tokens so users stay signed in and API requests can be authenticated. Until logout, account deletion, session expiry, or removal by the app/operating system.
Onboarding draft storage Stores setup progress, selected topics, selected country/language/region, briefing time, and onboarding completion status. Until onboarding is completed, the draft is cleared, or the app data is removed.
Notification preference storage Stores whether daily local reminders are enabled. Notifications are optional and can be disabled in the app or system settings. Until changed by the user, account reset, or removal of app data.
RevenueCat purchase data Used to configure purchases, subscriptions, premium entitlement status, purchase restoration, and app user identification for billing. For as long as needed to provide and verify purchases, subscriptions, and restored entitlements.
Bearer access token in API requests Sent in the Authorization header for protected app endpoints such as profile, preferences, briefings, and audio access. Only for the duration of the request on the client; server side logs may be retained for security and troubleshooting.
Briefing transcripts and audio Used to provide current and archived personalized briefings in text and audio form. For as long as needed to provide the briefing service, unless the account is deleted or deletion is required earlier.
Analytics or advertising cookies None found in the reviewed app code. Not applicable
Technical server logs Security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and delivery of presso.at and the Presso API. Kept only as long as necessary for these purposes, unless longer retention is legally required.
